Industry Landscape

Each bubble is a 2-digit NAICS industry in 2026 Q1; size reflects total employment. The horizontal split at 0% salary growth and the vertical split at 0% employment growth define four regions. Both growth rates are year over year. Industries expanding on both fronts (jobs and pay): Accommodation & Food Services, Construction, and Admin & Waste Services. Professional & Technical Services are losing both jobs and pay growth.

Source: BLS QCEW — Quarterly
